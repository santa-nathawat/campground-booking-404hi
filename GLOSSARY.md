# Glossary

- **Campground:** camping destination with a name, address, and telephone number. Use this term instead of hospital or hotel.
- **Booking:** a user's stay at a campground between check-in and check-out. Use this term instead of appointment.
- **Registered user:** a person registered with a name, telephone number, email, and password.
- **Booking owner:** the user the booking belongs to.
- **Administrator:** a user who can view, edit, and delete anyone's bookings. This does not mean campground owner.
- **Check-in date:** the date a stay begins.
- **Check-out date:** the date a stay ends. That night's stay is excluded.
- **Night:** one calendar day between check-in and check-out. December 1–4 is three nights.
